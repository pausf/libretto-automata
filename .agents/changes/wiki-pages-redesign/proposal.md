Tracker: none

## The ask, verbatim

> no me gusta el diseño me gusta que se mas minimalista y mas visual y que no
> sea una lista infinita si no que abra como paginas por cada spec con
> informacion clave de cuando se cambio etc.. hazlo todo eso en este pr

## Reading

Not a bug — a redesign of the viewer's information architecture, superseding parts
of modernize-wiki-design (also unreleased, same PR). Three demands:

1. **Not an infinite list** — a home view of capability cards, and one "page" per
   capability opened from it, instead of every section stacked on one scroll.
2. **More minimalist and more visual at once** — less ornament, information
   carried by cards and small marks rather than one big chart over a long page.
3. **Key info per spec** — when it last changed (from git), plus the counts the
   extraction already knows.

Delivered on PR #67, as asked.
